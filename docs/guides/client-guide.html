Your Re·Form app

Book your classes, keep track of your package and get the studio news, all from your phone. Here's how it works.

1Create your account

  1. Tap Secure your package on the home page (or Create account on the sign-in page).
  2. Fill in your name, surname, date of birth, email, mobile number and a password (twice). Classes are for adults: you need to be 18 or over.
  3. Choose whether we may share photos of you from class, accept the Studio Rules, Payments & Cancellation Policy and Terms, and tap Create account.
  4. We email you a short code. Type it in and tap Confirm. You only do this once.
  5. Answer the health questions. Only your instructor sees them, and only to keep you safe in class.

2Sign in, or forgot your password

Sign in with your email and password. Your phone can remember them for next time.

Forgot your password? Tap Forgot password?, enter your email, and we'll send you a code to set a new one.

If you booked with us before the app had passwords, use Forgot password? once to set yours.

3Choose a package

  1. Tap Secure your package. There's a tab for each kind of class (for example Reformer and Mat).
  2. Pick how many classes you'd like. A package lasts 30 or 60 days from your first class, so it doesn't start until you come.
  3. Have a discount code? Tap Have a code? and type it in. If the studio gave you a reward, it's already applied.
  4. Choose Cash or Revolut, read the package terms, tick the box and tap Request package.
  5. Pay your instructor. As soon as she confirms the payment, your classes appear in My classes and you can book.

New to Re·Form? Under the packages you'll also see a trial (your first class of that kind) and a drop-in (one class). Book those straight from a class on the schedule.

4Book a class

  1. Open Schedule and swipe through the days. You can book up to 4 weeks ahead.
  2. The colours: lavender means spots available, pink means you're booked, and pale pink means full (join the waitlist).
  3. Tap a class, then Secure your spot · 1 class. It's taken from your package and you get a confirmation email.
  4. No package yet? Choose Book your trial or Drop-in, pick cash or Revolut, and pay before class.
  5. Tap Add to calendar so you don't forget.
A Reformer package books Reformer classes, a Mat package books Mat classes, and so on. The class page tells you if your package isn't for that class.

5Same days every week

Always come on the same days? Open My classes, tap your package, then Book the same days every week. Pick your days and times and we'll book them every week while your package lasts.

If a class is full one week, we skip it and email you a link to join the waitlist. You can still cancel any single class as usual.

6Waitlist

When a class is full, tap Join the waitlist. You'll see your place ("You're 1st on the waitlist…"). If a spot opens more than 1 hour before class and you have a class left in your package, we book you in automatically and email you. In the last hour before class nobody is moved in.

7Cancelling

  • Open My classes, find the class under Upcoming and tap Cancel.
  • 6 hours or more before class: the class goes straight back into your package.
  • Less than 6 hours before, or not coming: the class is used. Please still cancel, so someone on the waitlist can take your spot.
  • If the studio cancels a class, your class always comes back to you.

8Events

Tap Events on the home page to see what's coming up (SUP Pilates, brunch and more). Events are paid individually and don't use your package.

  • Some events are for clients only: they show as "Clients only" until you have an active package.
  • Free events are booked with one tap.
  • Some events ask you a short question when you book (for example, dietary needs).
  • Events can't be cancelled in the app. If you can't make it, message the studio.

9My classes and Account

My classes shows your current package (how many classes you've used and how many are left, and when it ends), your upcoming classes, anything waiting for payment, and your past packages.

Account has drop-down sections:

  • Your details: name, mobile, date of birth, emergency contact, photo choice.
  • Health: update your answers any time (only your instructor sees them).
  • Receipts: a receipt for every payment.
  • Notifications: turn class reminders on or off.
  • Privacy & data: download your data or delete your account.

10Rules and policies

Tap Rules & policies on the home page, or the links at the bottom of any page, for the Studio Rules, the Payments & Cancellation Policy and the Terms. In short: arrive 5–10 minutes early, bring grip socks for Reformer, tell your instructor about any injury, condition or pregnancy, and packages are personal.
