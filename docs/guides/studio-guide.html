1Signing in

  • Use the same sign-in page as clients (or Studio sign-in at the bottom of any page) with your email and password. You land straight in your studio.
  • First time: tap Forgot password?, and we email you a code to set your password.
  • Client view ↗ (top right) shows the app exactly as clients see it.
  • Five tabs: Today · Classes · Clients · Stats · Settings. Every page except Today has a Back button.

2First set-up (once)

Open Settings. The checklist at the top shows what's left before your first client. Tap each one:

  1. Your studio: your name, address, phone and email for clients.
  2. How clients pay you: your Revolut link, IBAN and the name on the account, and the student discount.
  3. Booking rules: how many weeks ahead clients can book (4), the free-cancellation hours (6) and when the waitlist stops (60 minutes before class).
  4. Studio emails: the address that gets your emails, and which ones you want (section 9).
  5. Classes → Monthly timetable: your weekly classes (section 4).
  6. Photos and Your texts (section 9).

Then tap Save settings. One button saves every section.

3Today

Your day at a glance: today's classes with who's coming, the waitlist, and everything awaiting payment.

  • Flags next to names: Birthday, First class, Under 18, Trial, Drop-in, No photos / No face, and the heart for health answers (tap to read). Doctor's note needed stays until you tap Doctor's note received.
  • Mark as paid: when a client pays for a package, trial or drop-in, tap how they paid (Cash Revolut Bank Card). Package classes are added straight away and the client gets an email.
  • Remove: deletes a request that won't be paid. Unpaid requests also expire by themselves after 48 hours.

4Classes, events and the timetable

Find a class

Classes opens on the next day with classes. Swipe the days and tap a class to see its Clients: how each person booked, their flags and answers.

On a class page

  • Attendance (after class): set each person to Attended, No-show or Not marked. You can change it later.
  • Add a client: choose an existing client, or …or someone new (name, phone, email and a note). Then choose how they pay: settled outside the app, one of their package classes, not paid yet, or free. They take one of the spots like anyone else, so a full class stays full. To squeeze in an extra person, raise the spots first (Edit class). What you can do that clients can't: add someone beyond the booking window, after the 6-hour cutoff, or to a clients-only event or private session.
  • Edit class to change the time, spots, price or details, or cancel it. Clients are emailed and their classes come back to them.

A new class or an event

Classes → New class. Choose the class type, date, time, minutes and spots. For an event, choose the type Event and fill in:

  • Title and price (0 makes it free: booked with one tap, no payment).
  • Under Event options: Title font (calligraphy, handwritten, minimal, simple, elegant or bold, with a preview), Clients only, what to bring, a question for people booking, your own event terms, and a recap for afterwards.

On an event's page you can add a cover picture and photos, and let individual people see a clients-only event (Who can see this event).

Events you've already held

Add them the same way (Classes → New class → Event) with their real date in the past. They appear on the Events page under Previous events. To make them nicer, open the event and add photos (Event photos) and a few words under Event options → After the event.

Monthly timetable (repeats by itself)

Classes → Monthly timetable. Set your weekly classes once: they repeat every week by themselves. To change your pattern, edit it and save with the date it starts from. Booked classes are always kept.

How far ahead clients can book: Settings → Booking rules → Clients can book (weeks ahead): 4 is about a month, 13 is three months (up to 26). Your classes are always created at least 2 weeks further than that (never less than 9 weeks).

Holidays and closures

Classes → Holidays & closures. Confirm a suggested Cyprus public holiday, or add your own dates. Classes on those days are cancelled (clients are emailed and get their classes back), nobody can book them, and running packages are frozen for those days.

5Clients

Clients lists your Active clients first (paid package classes left, or a paid class coming up), then Not active, alphabetically, with their phone. The cake means it's their birthday today: open them and send your birthday email.

A client's page

  • Packages, attended classes and no-shows for each class type; their current package; details and your private notes; health answers (and earlier versions); student status.
  • Student: after seeing their card in person, set "Student until". They get the student price on group packages until then. No photo is stored.
  • Adjust package: extend the end date, freeze (From–To: no bookings with that package in between, and the end date moves by the same days), end earlier, add or remove classes, or give classes with their own end date (for example, a missed holiday class).
  • History, Payments and the Class ledger (every class added, used, returned or adjusted) are in the drop-downs at the bottom.

6Rewards and discount codes

A reward for one client

On their page → Rewards → Give a reward. Tick what it applies to (trial, drop-in, package, event) and choose what it gives: % off, € off, or free classes added to their next package. Add an end date if you like. It's emailed to them, used once, and applied by itself.

A code for anyone

Clients → Discount codes. Type the code (e.g. SUMMER10), tick what it applies to, choose what it gives, and add an end date or a total limit if you like. Clients type it under Have a code?. Each person can use a code once. Turn a code off any time.

A code doesn't add to the student discount: the client gets whichever is bigger. Free classes are added when you mark the package as paid.

7Class types, packages and single classes

Class types are what appear on your schedule and timetable (Reformer, Mat, Pre & Post Natal Mat, Private Reformer…). Each one says which packages pay for it. Packages and single classes are what clients buy, for one class type. The Packages page section sets the tabs clients see after "Secure your package" (Mat, Reformer, Pre and Post Natal, Private), which class types each tab holds, a note at the top, and whether clients can see it yet.

Already set up for you: Mat and Reformer (shown), Pre and Post Natal (Mat and Reformer packages, trials and drop-ins) and Private (Mat, Reformer and Pre & Post Natal Mat / Reformer based, with your descriptions and the note about arranging times). Pre and Post Natal and Private are hidden: to launch one, open Settings → Packages page → the tab → tick Show this tab to clients → Save. You can check a hidden tab first from Client view → Packages.
Private sessions never appear on the schedule and can't be booked online. When a client buys a private package, agree the time with them, then Classes → New class → the private type (1 spot) → open it → Add a client → "one of their package classes".

Example: adding Pre & post natal

  1. Settings → Packages & single classes → + Add a package or single class.
  2. Choose Single class, kind Trial, and name it "Pre and Post Natal Reformer Trial".
  3. At For, choose + New class type…. Name: Pre and post natal; Spots: 3; Minutes: 50; Classes used: 1; default location and description. Tap Save: it's now chosen in "For" and listed under Class types.
  4. Enter the price (and a short description if you like), choose whether it's on sale, and tap Save single class.
  5. Add a Drop-in the same way, then Packages (For: Pre and post natal; classes, months, days valid, price).
  6. Add the class to your Monthly timetable or as a New class.

Clients now see a Pre and post natal tab on Packages, with its packages, trial and drop-in.

Changing or deleting

  • Class types: tap one to change its name, which packages pay for it, spots, minutes, classes used (e.g. 2 for a private), location and description. Untick Active to hide it from the timetable and new classes. Delete removes it from your timetable and lists. You can't delete one with classes still to come: cancel or change those first. Past classes keep it in clients' history.
  • Packages and single classes: tap one to change the price, classes, months, days valid or description. Untick On sale to hide it. Delete removes it; clients who already bought it keep it, and it stays on their receipts.
  • A private session that uses 2 classes: add a class type "Private" paid with Reformer packages and set Classes used to 2. Or give it its own packages, as in the example above.

8Stats

  • This week: bookings out of spots, the fill rate, and fill rate by day.
  • Month (drop-down): bookings, fill rate, revenue, new clients, each class type side by side, and package classes used and expired.
  • Year (drop-down) and Show: one bar per month for revenue, packages sold, fill rate, bookings or new clients, plus payments by method with Export CSV for your accountant.

9Settings, texts and photos

Studio emails

Settings → Studio emails. You get an email when a client books (it says First booking when a class was empty, so you know an early class is on), creates an account, cancels, requests a package, or changes their details or health answers. Untick any you don't want. Changes you make yourself don't email you, and health answers are never put in an email.

Your texts

Settings → Your texts: the home page intro line, Studio Rules, Payments & Cancellation Policy, What to bring, The Studio text, the Events intro and the Terms & Conditions. In the Terms, start a line with ## for a heading and - for a bullet point. Empty it and save to go back to our default.

More in Settings

  • Photos: your logo, studio photos and event pictures (only your own or licensed photos).
  • Message on every page: a banner for news, e.g. a closed week.
  • Studio team: who can open the studio side.
  • Messages: every email the app has sent.
  • Import: clients from a spreadsheet.
  • Launch checklist: what to tick before turning on card payments.

11The rules the app follows

  • Cancelling: 6 hours or more before class, the class goes back into the same package with its original end date. Later cancellations and no-shows use the class (the spot goes to the waitlist).
  • Packages: paid in advance and valid 30 or 60 days from the first class. Classes can't be booked after the end date; unused classes expire. Packages are personal and don't renew automatically.
  • Trials: only for someone who has never booked that class type, checked by account and phone number. Trials and drop-ins reserve the spot to pay before class, one unpaid class at a time.
  • Waitlist: a spot that opens more than 1 hour before class books the first person who has a package class left, and emails them. In the last hour nobody is moved in.
  • Studio-cancelled classes always come back to the client. Closures freeze running packages.
  • Events are paid individually and can't be cancelled by clients in the app.
  • Health answers are seen only by you and are never emailed. Clients must be 18 or over.
