## Who can book

Our classes are for adults aged 18 or over. Each account is for one person: please keep your password to yourself.

## How a booking or package is agreed

- Packages: you send a request in the app and we show you how to pay, with your reference (RF-…). The package starts once we mark your payment as received. A request that isn't paid within 48 hours expires.
- Single classes, trials, drop-ins and events: booking reserves your spot for the class, date and time shown, and you pay before class.
- You can book up to 4 weeks ahead. Each booking is for one person.

## Prices and payment

- Prices are in euro and are the total price you pay.
- Pay in advance by cash, Revolut or bank transfer (the details are shown when you request a package or book).
- You receive a payment confirmation (receipt). It isn't a tax invoice.
- Students get 10% off group packages once we've checked their student card in person. Discount codes and rewards don't add up with the student price: the lower price applies.

## Packages

- Each package gives a number of classes for a class type (for example, Reformer). Events are paid individually and don't use package classes.
- A package is valid for 30 or 60 days from the date of your first class, depending on the package. Classes can't be booked beyond the expiry date, and classes not used by then expire.
- Packages are personal and can't be transferred or shared.
- Packages don't renew automatically. When yours is used up or about to end, choose a new one in the app under Packages.
- Packages are frozen while the studio is closed (for example at Christmas, Easter and in the summer) and during a freeze we agree with you: the end date moves by the same number of days.

## Refunds

- Once a package, class or event is paid, it isn't refundable, except when the studio cancels (see below).

## Cancelling a class

- 6 hours or more before class: cancel in the app and the class goes back into the same package, with its original expiry date.
- Less than 6 hours before class, or not coming: the class is used. Please still cancel, so your spot goes to the waitlist.
- The waitlist: if a spot opens more than 1 hour before class, the first person waiting who has a package class is booked in automatically and emailed, and these cancellation rules apply to that booking.
- Events can't be cancelled in the app. If you can't come, message the studio.

## Changes and cancellations by the studio

We may need to cancel or move a class (for example because of illness). If we cancel a class, the class always comes back into your package so you can book another time, even if you had already cancelled it late. If that leaves you too little time to use it before your package ends, we may extend your package. If we cancel an event, you get a full refund. Apart from these cases, unused or expired classes aren't refunded.
