# Re·Form app: try it out 💜

Hi Elysia! Your booking app is almost ready for real clients. This link is the **practice version**: made-up clients, no real payments, no emails. Try anything; you can't break it.

**Open it on your phone:** https://reform-pilates-seven.vercel.app
Tip: in your browser menu choose **Add to Home Screen** to use it like an app. It updates by itself.

## What's new
- **Your notes, done:** your logo, **Secure your package** with your prices, Cash or Revolut, Trial and Drop-in, booking **the same days every week**, three colours (pink booked, purple spots left, light pink full), My classes with progress like 6/8, Studio · Events · Rules circles.
- **Safer sign-up:** new clients give their **date of birth** and answer **8 quick health questions** (the standard PAR-Q, plus pregnancy). Any "yes" and they're told to check with their doctor and tell you.
- **Waitlist books itself:** if a spot opens **more than 6 hours** before class, the first person waiting is booked automatically with a class from their package, and emailed. Closer to class, the spot is held for them for 2 hours instead.
- **Clearer emails:** every booking email says **until when they can cancel for free** (6h before). Clients can turn off the day-before reminder in their Account.

## 1. Try it as a client (4 minutes)
1. Tap **Secure your package**, choose Reformer or Mat and how many times a week.
2. Sign in with **any email**. In the practice version the code fills itself in: just tap **Continue**.
3. Fill in your details once: name, phone, **date of birth** and the **health questions**. Try answering "yes" to one to see what clients see.
4. Choose **Cash** or **Revolut**, tick the box and **Request package**.
5. Mark it paid in the studio part (step 2), then come back and book from the **Schedule**, or tap **Every week** on the home page.
6. No package? Open any class and try **Book your trial**.
7. Cancel a class from **My classes** and see it go back into your package.

**Want to see a regular client?** Sign in as `elena@example.com`: a current package, a finished one (5/8) and receipts.

## 2. Run your studio (4 minutes)
1. On the same link, sign in with **elysia.stephani@gmail.com** and the **studio password** we sent you separately.
2. **Today:**
   - **Awaiting payment:** you see how each client said they'll pay. Tap how they paid (or **Remove** if they're not coming) and their classes appear.
   - Next to names: *Birthday*, *First class*, *Trial*, *Drop-in*, *Under 18*, *No photos*, *PAR-Q: yes* (tap *Health info* to read it; only you can see it).
   - Tap the circle to check someone in.
3. **Classes:** add someone from your DMs, remove someone, or cancel a class (everyone is told and gets their class back). Next month in **Monthly timetable**; holidays in **Holidays & closures**.
4. **Clients:** details, emergency contact, notes; give a class back; mark a student (10% off).
5. **Settings** starts with **Before your first client**: what's still missing. Below it: **Photos**, **Package prices**, **Your texts**, **Studio team**.
6. **Settings → Messages:** every email the app sends, and **Automatic reminders: last ran a few minutes ago** (they go out on their own every 5 minutes).
7. **Settings → Reset demo data** puts the practice version back to the start.
