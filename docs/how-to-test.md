# Re·Form app: try it out 💜

Hi Elysia! This is a first version of your booking app. Nothing here is real yet: no payments, no emails, made-up clients. Play with it as much as you like and tell us what you think.

**Open it on your phone:** https://reform-pilates-seven.vercel.app
Tip: in your browser menu choose **Add to Home Screen** to use it like an app.

---

## 1. Book as a client (3 minutes)
1. Tap **Secure your spot**, pick a day and a class.
2. Sign in with **any email**. In the demo the code fills itself in, so just tap **Continue**.
3. Fill in your details once.
4. You'll be asked to get a package. Open **Packages**, tap **Choose**, tick the box and **Request package**. You'll see your payment info (cash / Revolut / bank).
5. Leave this open and do step 2 below to "receive" your payment. Then come back and book a class.

**Want to see a regular client?** Sign in as `elena@example.com`. She already has classes, bookings and a "book your usual" button.

## 2. Run your studio (3 minutes)
1. On the same link, sign in with **elysia.stephani@gmail.com** and the **studio password** we sent you separately.
2. **Today:**
   - **Awaiting payment:** tap how they paid (Cash, Revolut, Bank or Card). Their classes appear instantly.
   - Today's classes. Tap the circle to check people in.
3. **Classes:**
   - Open a class to add someone from your DMs, remove someone, or cancel the class (everyone is told and gets their class back).
   - Set up next month in **Monthly timetable**, and close the studio for holidays in **Holidays & closures**.
4. **Clients:** notes, give a class back, mark a student (10%).
5. **Settings → Your texts:** change your rules, policy and the "about you" text yourself.
6. **Settings → Reset demo data** puts everything back to start.
