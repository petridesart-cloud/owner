# How to test Re·Form (for Elysia)

Thank you for trying it! This is a **demo**: payments are simulated, no emails or texts are sent, and nothing here is real yet. Use your phone and open the link from Instagram or WhatsApp, the same way your clients would.

## As a client (about 5 minutes)
1. Open the link. You land on **Home**. Tap **Secure your spot**.
2. Pick a day in the date strip and tap a class.
   - "1 of 3 reformers left" means almost full.
   - **Full** means you can join the waitlist.
3. Tap **Secure your spot** again and enter **any email**. The demo shows your 6-digit code on screen (normally it arrives by email). Enter it.
4. First time only: add your name and phone, tick the Studio Rules and health declaration, and optionally add a health note.
5. Like your real policy, everything is paid in advance. You'll be asked to **Get a package**. Open **Packages**, tap **Choose** on one, tick the terms and **Request package**.
   - You'll see how to pay: cash, Revolut or bank transfer, with a reference code.
   - As the studio (below), you'll mark it paid, and the classes appear.
6. With classes in your account, open a class and tap **Secure your spot · 1 class**. You'll see the confirmation and, for a first class, what to expect. You can **Add to calendar**.
7. Open **My classes** (bottom bar) and cancel the booking. It's more than 6 hours away, so the class goes back into your package.
8. Find a **Full** class and tap **Join the waitlist**. You'll see your place in line.
9. Try **Account**: edit details, download your data, and see your consents.

**Returning client:** sign in as `elena@example.com`. She has an 8-class package that started at her first class, past classes, a booking on Tuesday and a "Your usual" rebook card.

## As the studio (about 5 minutes)
1. Sign in with your admin email. Instead of a code, enter the **admin code** you were given.
2. **Today:**
   - **Awaiting payment:** tap Cash / Revolut / Bank / Card when someone pays. Their classes are added.
   - Your classes and who's coming. Tap the circle to check someone in. Health notes sit behind a tap.
3. **Classes:** tap a class to:
   - add a DM client manually;
   - remove someone (their credit comes back);
   - change capacity;
   - cancel the class (everyone is notified and credited).
4. **Timetable:** set next month's weekly times, then **Save & create**.
5. **Clients:** search, add private notes, adjust classes (with a reason), mark someone as a verified student (no card photos stored) or give a class back for a public holiday.
6. **Closures:** confirm the suggested public holidays or add your Christmas, Easter and Summer breaks. Classes those days are cancelled and packages are frozen.
7. **Outbox:** every email and text the app would have sent (confirmations, reminders, waitlist offers).
8. **Stats**, **Settings** (cutoff hours, prices, VAT, business details) and **Compliance** (launch checklist).
9. **Settings → Reset demo data** puts everything back to fresh demo data.
