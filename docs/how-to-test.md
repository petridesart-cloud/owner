## 1. Book as a client (3 minutes)
1. Tap **Secure your package**, pick Reformer or Mat and how many times a week.
2. Sign in with **any email**. In the demo the code fills itself in, so just tap **Continue**, then fill in your details once.
3. Choose **Cash** or **Revolut**, tick the box and **Request package**.
4. Mark it paid in the studio part (step 2 below), then come back: book from the **Schedule**, or tap **Every week** on the home page.
5. Or open any class without a package and try **Book your trial**.

**Want to see a regular client?** Sign in as `elena@example.com`: a current package, a finished one (5/8) and receipts.

## 2. Run your studio (3 minutes)
1. On the same link, sign in with **elysia.stephani@gmail.com** and the **studio password** we sent you separately.
2. **Today:**
   - **Awaiting payment:** you see how each client said they'll pay; tap how they paid. Their classes appear instantly.
   - Names show **First class**, **Trial**, **Drop-in** and **No photos**.
   - Today's classes. Tap the circle to check people in.
3. **Classes:**
   - Open a class to add someone from your DMs, remove someone, or cancel the class (everyone is told and gets their class back).
   - Set up next month in **Monthly timetable**, and close the studio for holidays in **Holidays & closures**.
4. **Clients:** notes, give a class back, mark a student (10%).
5. **Settings → Photos:** your logo, photos for each room, your portrait and event photos. **Package prices** are a simple grid.
6. **Settings → Your texts:** change your rules, policy and the "about you" text yourself.
7. **Settings → Reset demo data** puts everything back to start.
